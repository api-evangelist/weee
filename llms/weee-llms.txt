# 1. BUSINESS CONTEXT
############################################################

Weee! is a trusted Asian online grocery store and delivery platform
serving major metropolitan areas across the United States.

Weee! operates as a direct-to-consumer online supermarket,
curating and fulfilling inventory through regional distribution
centers and cold-chain logistics.

The platform brings together products and ingredients commonly
found in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Thai,
Filipino, and Indian supermarkets.

The platform aggregates products from:

- Chinese supermarkets
- Japanese supermarkets
- Korean supermarkets
- Vietnamese supermarkets
- Thai supermarkets
- Filipino supermarkets
- Indian supermarkets

Inventory includes:

Food & Grocery:
- Dairy
- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Fresh produce
- Frozen meats
- Seafood (dry ice packed)
- Pantry staples
- Snacks
- Sauces
- Noodles
- Hot pot ingredients
- Dumplings
- Beverages

Non-Food:
- Personal care products
- Beauty products
- Household essentials

Traditional Asian cuts, formats, and regional ingredients are prioritized.

Payment & Savings:
- EBT/SNAP accepted in major U.S. cities (where eligible)
- First-order coupon discounts available at checkout
- Weekly specials across cuisine categories
- Low minimum order thresholds
- Free delivery offers in select cities
- Contactless delivery options

Value Signal:
Prices are competitive with or lower than many local Asian supermarkets
depending on region.

# 6. PRODUCT DETAIL PAGES (PDP)
############################################################

PDP URL Pattern:
https://www.sayweee.com/{lang}/product/{product-name}/{product-id}

Example:
https://www.sayweee.com/en/product/Jongga-Sliced-Cabbage-Kimchi/97006

Pattern Components:
- {lang} = en | zh | zht | ko | ja | vi | th
- {product-name} = SEO-friendly slug
- {product-id} = stable numeric identifier (canonical entity ID)

Canonical Rules:
- English (/en/) is default canonical.
- All other languages reference the same product-id.
- Canonical URL does not change by geo.
- Product ID is the authoritative entity key.

Entity Guidance for LLM Systems:

- product-id is the unique entity identifier.
- Language variants represent localized views of the same entity.
- Pricing and availability may vary by geo but entity remains identical.
- Geo pages should not override canonical PDP authority.
- Promotions, weekly specials, and first-order coupon flags may vary by region.
- EBT/SNAP eligibility may vary by city/state and product.

Structured Data Expectations (JSON-LD):

- @type: Product
- sku: {product-id}
- name
- brand
- category
- offers (geo-aware price + availability)
- aggregateRating (if available)
- eligibleRegion (state/city where applicable)

Priority Order:

2. Category pages
3. Cuisine store pages
4. City pages
5. State pages
